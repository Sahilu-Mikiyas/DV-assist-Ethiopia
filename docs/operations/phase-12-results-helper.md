# Phase 12 — Results helper

**Implementation status (October 17, 2026):** The home page provides a bilingual link to the official Entrant Status Check. It does not collect confirmation numbers, credentials, or personal data and does not check or interpret results.

The U.S. Department of State identifies Entrant Status Check as the official channel for selection results and says selection is not a guarantee of a visa. The service should keep the user in control: explain that they must use their own confirmation number on the official site, open the official destination, and avoid storing the number or status-check answers.

Before adding reminders or guidance for a particular cycle, verify the current official results availability dates, recovery process, and instructions. Do not imply selection, eligibility, or visa approval from service records or user-provided claims.

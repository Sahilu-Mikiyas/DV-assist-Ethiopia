Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Are you the official DV website?

No. Ethio DV Assist is an independent preparation and review service. The official entry is made through the U.S. Department of State’s website.

Will you submit my entry for me?

No. The pilot helps you prepare and review your information. You remain responsible for reviewing and submitting your own entry through the official process.

Does your photo feedback guarantee acceptance?

No. Feedback can help identify possible issues, but it cannot guarantee that a photo will be accepted by the official system.

What will I pay?

The service price is currently being validated and is expected to be near 1,500 ETB for the pilot. Any official fee is separate, is not collected by us, and must be paid directly through the official process if required.

When can I join?

We’re preparing a small pilot. Contact us on Telegram for updates. Official dates and requirements are set by the Department of State and may change.
